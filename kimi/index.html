CASE 01 / CORE PROJECT

From 100+ student enquiries
to an open-source AI study workflow

Students kept asking where to start, how to identify gaps and how to balance online lessons with practice, and how to make AI part of their study routine.

01 / WHERE IT STARTED

One post on high school study systems reached over 120,000 views. I then identified recurring questions in comments and messages, structured 81 complete records and recruited five students for the initial in-depth trial.

02 / WHAT I DID
  • Anonymised comments, messages and follow-up feedback
  • Designed structured intake and follow-up questions
  • Used AI to draft four-week paths and seven-day starter plans
  • Reviewed each plan manually, recording edits and errors
  • Collected daily evidence and adjusted the next step
  • Open-sourced rules, prompts, templates and anonymised cases
FROM QUESTION TO CORRECTION 8 STEPS
  1. 01 Real student questions
  2. 02 Structured intake
  3. 03 AI follow-up questions
  4. 04 Initial path triage
  5. 05 Four-week overview
  6. 06 Daily tasks
  7. 07 Evidence from practice
  8. 08 Ongoing adjustments

SYSTEM / WHAT SHIPPED

What the project includes

A working structure with input checks, human judgement and feedback-based adjustments, beyond a single prompt.

  • 01Student intake protocol
  • 02Input completeness checks
  • 03Study paths and priorities
  • 04Four-week path and seven-day starter templates
  • 05Maths, English, physics and chemistry rules
  • 06Daily feedback and adjustments
  • 07Human review and risk boundaries
  • 08Manual prompts and open-source repository

EVIDENCE / NOT A DEMO

Plans need to meet real life.

I retain anonymised feedback on time spent, friction, unfinished tasks and decisions needing human judgement, alongside successful outputs.

THREE FINDINGS

Generating a plan is the easy part. Getting enough reliable information is harder.
Personalisation means changing the next step based on what happened in practice.
The system must know when to stop automated judgement and ask a person for help.
